# Home: the jukebox

Mode: Experience. Audience: Kat's friends opening a shared link, mostly on laptops. Job: see the games, pick one, play it in one click.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one jukebox cabinet at night. The game list is the record selector itself, never a grid of cards with a hero above it.

OWN-WORLD: Warm night ground (deep plum-maroon floor glow). Walnut wood cabinet with a tall Wurlitzer arch, chrome trim, neon tubes in orange, hot pink and yellow. Records are black vinyl with grooves and a printed center label. The info panel is a cream jukebox title strip with colored bands. Type: Monoton for the neon marquee, Bungee for codes and signage, Figtree for reading text.

STORY: The visitor sees Kat's arcade, understands each record is a game, flicks through, and presses the big push button to play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cabinet centered, arch on top holding the "Kitty's Arcade" neon marquee. Below it the glass record window with the selected record front and center, two neighbors each side angled away. Chunky chrome left and right buttons flank the window. Under the window, the title strip (selection code, title, description) and the big rounded Play push button, all visible at 1440x900 without scrolling.

FORM: Pinned by the user's brief (a 1980s jukebox in an arcade at night). concept-seed was deliberately not run because a pinned direction beats the roll. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the record flick. Drag or swipe with momentum, arrow keys and buttons; neighbors swing in perspective on Arc's spring curve, the selected record turns slowly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
